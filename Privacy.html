Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Shubhanshi Mishra, trading as Analyst Shubhi (“we”, “us”), collects through https://techwithme.streamlit.app/ (the “Website”), why we collect it, and how we handle it. By using the Website you agree to this policy.

1. What we collect

  • When you buy or book: your name and email address, the item you chose, and for calls the time slot you picked. For Priority DM, the question you type.
  • Payment details: payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank login. We receive only a confirmation with your payment ID, the amount, the payment status and the contact details you entered at checkout.
  • Free resources and newsletter: your name and email when you ask for a free resource or sign up to the mailing list.
  • Messages to us: anything you send to our email address, including the contents of your message.
  • Technical data: our hosting providers may log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to keep the Website running.

2. How we use it

  • to deliver what you bought: send your access link or booking confirmation, set up and update your call invitation and meeting link, and answer your Priority DM;
  • to confirm and keep a record of payments, and to handle support requests and refunds;
  • to remind you about, or reschedule, your booked call;
  • to send you our newsletter, only if you signed up. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email;
  • to meet our accounting, tax and legal duties, and to prevent fraud and misuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who processes your information

We use trusted services to run the Website. They handle your information only to provide their service to us:

  • Razorpay for payments;
  • Google (Sheets, Gmail, Calendar and Meet) to keep order records, send emails and manage calls;
  • Streamlit Community Cloud and Render to host the Website and its payment-confirmation service;
  • GitHub to host this policy and our other legal pages.

Some of these providers store data on servers outside India. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights.

4. How long we keep it

We keep order and payment records for as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal purposes. Other information, such as newsletter sign-ups and support emails, is kept only as long as it is useful for the purpose it was collected for, or until you ask us to delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it.

5. Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The Website and our hosting providers may use basic technical storage that is necessary for the pages to work.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent to the newsletter at any time. Email us from the address you used at checkout and we will respond within 48 hours on working days. We may keep what the law requires us to keep, such as payment records.

7. Security

We keep our records in access-protected accounts and use the security features of the services above. No method of storing or sending data over the internet is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The Website is not meant for people under 18. If you are under 18, please use it only with a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version applies from the date shown at the top of this page.
