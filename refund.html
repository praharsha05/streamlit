Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short: all sales are final. Calls can be rescheduled. We refund only in a few exceptional cases, listed below, and we reply to every request within 48 hours on working days.

1. Digital products

Digital products are delivered immediately by email, so we cannot take them back. We do not give refunds, returns or exchanges after the access link has been sent. This includes change of mind, accidental purchases, lack of technical knowledge, and device or software incompatibility.

We will refund in these cases:

  • Duplicate payment: you were charged more than once for the same item.
  • Technical defect: the file does not work as described and we cannot fix it within 7 business days of your request.
  • Not as described: the product is materially different from its description on the Website.

2. 1:1 calls

  • Rescheduling: you can reschedule a booked call up to 2 times, using the link in your confirmation email, until 12 hours before the call.
  • Cancellation: a booked call is not cancelled for a refund. Please reschedule instead.
  • No-show or late change: if you miss the call, or try to reschedule less than 12 hours before it, the session counts as delivered and is not refunded or rebooked.
  • If we cancel: we will reschedule the call to a time that suits you, at no extra charge.
  • If your slot cannot be confirmed: if you are charged but we cannot confirm a time for you, and we cannot agree a new time with you, we will refund the full amount.

3. Priority DM

Once we have answered your question, the service is delivered and is not refunded. If we are unable to answer your question, we will refund the amount in full.

4. Payment problems

  • Payment failed, money deducted: failed payments are normally returned to your account automatically by your bank or Razorpay. If you do not see the money back after 7 business days, contact us with your payment details.
  • Paid, but no email: first check your Spam and Promotions folders. If nothing arrives within 30 minutes, contact us. We will resend your access or fix your booking.

5. How to ask for a refund

Email misshubhanshi@gmail.com within 7 days of your purchase, with:

  • the email address you used at checkout;
  • the name of the item and the date of purchase;
  • your Razorpay payment ID (it starts with “pay_” and is in your payment receipt), or a screenshot of the payment;
  • a short description of the problem.

We will reply within 48 hours on working days.

6. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method (card, UPI or bank account). We start each approved refund within 5 business days. After that, your bank or card issuer may take a further 5 to 7 business days to show the credit.
